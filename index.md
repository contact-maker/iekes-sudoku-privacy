# Iekes Sudoku Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 4 May 2026*

## Summary
Iekes Sudoku does not collect any data from you. The app runs entirely on
your device. Nothing leaves your iPhone or iPad.

## What we don't do
- We do not collect personal information
- We do not track your behavior
- We do not show ads
- We do not share data with third parties
- We do not connect to any analytics or telemetry service
- We do not require an account, an email address, or any login

## What we store, and where
The app stores the following on your device only — never transmitted anywhere:
- Your current Sudoku game (the puzzle and your moves)
- Your past game results (score, time, mistakes, won/lost) for the statistics page

This data is stored locally using Apple's SwiftData framework. It is not synced
to iCloud, sent to any server, or shared with anyone, including the developer.

## Your rights
You can erase all stored data at any time by opening Settings (the gear icon
on the home screen) and tapping "Reset all data". Uninstalling the app also
deletes all data.
